## Core Metrics

Define and distinguish:

- Gross sales
- Line discounts
- Total discount
- Sales after discount
- Tax and duties
- Net payable/net sales
- Returns
- Cost
- Gross profit when cost is available
- Target
- Achievement percentage
- Coverage
- Active customers
- Invoice count
- Average invoice
- Active brands/products
- Collection/receivables
- Commission

## Commission Rules

Commission is high risk. Before changing:

1. Locate current formula source.
2. Identify period and version.
3. Identify sales/return signs.
4. Identify target and achievement caps/floors.
5. Identify customer-type coefficients.
6. Identify brand, coverage, and return adjustments.
7. Identify shared-visitor rules.
8. Compare output against a trusted sample.

Do not replace an Excel formula with code until equivalence is demonstrated.
